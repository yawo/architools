## obsidian-second-brain

**Repository:** [eugeniughelbur/obsidian-second-brain](https://github.com/eugeniughelbur/obsidian-second-brain)
**Description:** Cross-CLI skill for Obsidian that turns your vault into a living AI-first second brain.
**Key Features:**
- **43+ Commands**: Includes /obsidian-save, /obsidian-daily, /obsidian-architect, and more.
- **AI-First Vault Rules**: Notes are structured for machine retrieval (recency markers, wikilinks, metadata).
- **Automated Documentation**: /obsidian-architect scans codebases to build maintained architecture notes.
- **Key-less Research**: Fallback to free sources for web research if no API keys are provided.
- **Cross-Platform**: Works across Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, and OpenCode.

**Primary Use Cases:**
- Managing a personal knowledge base with AI assistance.
- Automatically documenting software projects within an Obsidian vault.
- Conducting research and synthesizing knowledge directly into your second brain.
